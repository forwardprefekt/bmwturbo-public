# Rights, privacy, and source availability

## Scope of this edition

The owner requested a separate curated public research edition. This does not change the private archive's preservation/access rules or authorize distribution of that archive, its history, or its raw inputs. No repository has been pushed or made public as part of preparation.

## Licensing and third-party boundaries

Original documentation and research-report expression are licensed under CC BY 4.0; original software and software examples are licensed under MIT. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) for scope, attribution, and full license texts. These grants apply only to rights held by the contributors. Confirm ownership and contributor permissions before publishing. Third-party firmware, definitions, supplier information, quotations, and other source material are not relicensed merely because they informed the analysis. This notice is not legal clearance.

Firmware assembly listings and reconstructed firmware C are omitted pending a specific rights decision. Original mathematical explanations and source-bound conclusions remain, with their evidence limits. Attribution, provenance, transformations, or removal of personal information do not by themselves create redistribution permission. If a copied passage or table is identified, review or remove it before release; do not assume the author's interpretation of a source makes the source text freely distributable.

## Excluded artifacts

- Full DME reads, calibration/program binaries, flash packages and patches.
- Ghidra project databases, prepared loader binaries, and whole-program code exports.
- Third-party A2L/XDF definitions, EDIABAS/diagnostic programs, and OEM/standards PDFs.
- Private supplier workbooks, delivered-set records, correspondence, and source photos.
- Raw CAN/vehicle captures, account identifiers, DME serials, home paths, and private Git history.

Use legitimate upstream references or independently supplied authorized inputs when reproducing research. Do not request restricted source uploads to public issues.

## Evidence limits

A public findings report is a reviewed summary of private source-bound research, not a substitute for the withheld instructions or original measurements. Some reports describe evidence retained in the private archive. File names, source hashes, and addresses in those reports are provenance locators, not public download links. Omitted evidence must remain explicit; no passing link or privacy check proves firmware semantics, legal clearance, or engine safety.

## Privacy review limits

Automated scans cover common names, contact/identifier patterns, local paths, credentials, and unexpected artifact types. They are defense in depth, not a guarantee against all identifying details or indirect re-identification. Review every future public addition and its history independently.
