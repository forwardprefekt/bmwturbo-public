## Question

Can MSV70 use a truthful higher-range MAF measurement to calculate boosted
cylinder air mass, and can the existing spark, torque, and lambda tables be
extended from that calculated load?

### Issue #86 range-propagation conclusion

The **1023.984375 kg/h ceiling belongs to the Q6 transfer-table encoding**,
not to every downstream airflow or shared charge state. The executable
averager converts Q6 to **Q5** (`sum / (2 * count)`); corrected airflow,
its unsigned PT1 output, and the direct measured-load producer can represent
more than 1024 kg/h without globally changing those downstream units.
Widening a value **after** the original table has clipped cannot recover the
lost measurement. A local table-reencoding/divisor redesign remains a code
design candidate, not an implemented or validated calibration.

The consumer audit identifies two different shared coordinates:

- `6ab6`: adjusted **relative load**, halfword storage, normally 0.01%/count.
  There are 36 signed `LHA` reads and one unsigned `LHZ` read.
- `2ada`: **cylinder air charge**, unsigned halfword at the firmware/XDF
  convention of 0.021195 mg/stk/count, derived from `u16(6ab6)`.

Neither is a universal kg/h state. Separate limits still matter:

1. Direct measured-load normalization saturates at `32767` (327.67% before
   correction); charge conversion reaches the `0xffff` saturation sentinel
   already at load raw **21485**, or **214.85%**, with stock `c_maf_ref`.
2. The **optional pressure-integrator route** has its own signed-Q5 inflow
   clamp: `6c72 <= 32767`, equivalent to **1023.96875 kg/h total inflow**.
   Selecting measured airflow with `CW_EISY` bit 1 does not bypass that clamp.
   The direct bit-0 measured-load producer does bypass it.
3. Spark, torque, lambda and other consumers retain narrower calibration
   domains and independent arithmetic/diagnostic limits. Numeric headroom is
   not high-load calibration coverage or proof of correct physical charge.

Canonical `CW_EISY=0`, `S_RFST_ON=0`, and `S_EVNN_AUS=0` select modeled
load and leave the optional measured-flow integrator input inactive. Upstream
acquisition availability, selected-flow eligibility and update cadence are
separate conditions; setting the load-source bit does not force them.

The issue-#86 evidence directory (supporting artifact withheld from public edition)
contains the typed state inventory, explicit transitions, three consumer
audits, canonical selector-pointer bytes, fresh C/instruction exports,
source hashes, and a reproducible integer range experiment. Engineering units
remain firmware/XDF-consistent interpretations requiring physical validation.
This is analysis only: no image patch, calibration change, sensor substitution,
new DME read, or vehicle/bench execution.

## 1. Physical MAF signal to measured airflow

`00432fc8.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) reads ADC
logical channel `0x1b` through `FUN_0041f184`. It shifts the returned value by
two bits, then stores the low eight bits at `1f40`; the byte is split into
two four-bit indices into a 16 by 16 table. This is truncation, not an ADC
out-of-range clamp:

> Firmware implementation excerpt withheld from the public edition pending
> an explicit rights decision. The surrounding authored analysis is retained;
> this omission is not replacement instruction-level evidence.
Runtime address `0x849ca4` is the value component of `id_maf_tab`. The XDF
defines its unsigned 16-bit output at `0.015625 kg/h` per count. Consequently,
the largest truthful value this stock representation can report is:

```text
65535 * 0.015625 = 1023.984375 kg/h
```

A higher-range sensor or larger characterized housing can prevent the physical
sensor from saturating, but it cannot make this particular output represent
more than 1023.984375 kg/h without coordinated rescaling or code changes.

### Decoded stock transfer and scale headroom

Direct big-endian decoding of the 256 `uint16` cells at file offset `0x49ca4`
in the canonical
`canonical external-program image` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition)
shows a monotonic transfer from 0 to 65535 counts. The first 65535 cell is ADC
index 233; indices 233 through 255 are all saturated. Thus the stock table
reaches the fixed-point ceiling before the end of its eight-bit input domain.
A new sensor curve cannot truthfully extend mass flow by merely replacing
those saturated cells.

The related calibration limits provide useful voltage-domain boundaries, but
not a complete sensor characterization:

| Calibration | Decoded value | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| `c_v_maf_mafm_avl_min` | 0.800771 V | MAF availability lower boundary |
| `c_v_maf_mafm_avl_max` | 4.49213 V | MAF availability upper boundary |
| `c_v_maf_min_diag` | 0.507806 V | diagnostic lower boundary |
| `c_v_maf_max_diag` | 4.49213 V | diagnostic upper boundary |

These values come from
`msv70_calibration_components.csv` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition).
They do not prove a linear voltage-to-index mapping or the transfer curve of a
replacement MAF/housing. `ip_fac_maf_kgh_cor` also has an airflow axis ending
at 800 kg/h; above that breakpoint the ordinary interpolation behavior holds
the final correction cell rather than extrapolating a new correction.

### Idealized airflow screening envelope

Using the decoded `c_maf_ref = 646.511085 mg/stk` as an NA reference and
scaling only by ideal absolute-pressure ratio gives the following screening
values:

| Gauge boost | Ideal pressure ratio | Air mass | Airflow at 6000 rpm | Airflow at 7000 rpm |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 psi | 1.000 | 646.5 mg/stk | 698 kg/h | 815 kg/h |
| 5 psi | 1.340 | 866.4 mg/stk | 936 kg/h | 1092 kg/h |
| 8 psi | 1.544 | 998.4 mg/stk | 1078 kg/h | 1258 kg/h |

For the six-cylinder four-stroke engine, the tabulated flow uses
`kg/h = mg/stk * rpm * 0.00018`. This is a representation-risk screen, not a
dyno prediction: it omits compressor heating, intercooler effectiveness,
volumetric-efficiency changes, pressure loss, throttle loss, and cam/Valvetronic
state. It nevertheless shows that the stock 1023.984375 kg/h representation
can be exceeded at plausible boosted high-rpm operation even when the physical
sensor itself has not saturated.

### Rescaling consequence

A coordinated fixed-point rescale can buy numerical range. For example, with
`K = 1.25`, storing `internal = actual / K` maps 1258 kg/h to 1006.4 internal
kg/h. That change is not local to `id_maf_tab`: unmodified consumers would see
airflow 20% low. The current whole-program inventory finds 35 direct reads of
selected airflow `r13+0x2b30` across 23 functions; the issue-#86 census retains
the remaining unclassified readers explicitly. A safe global rescale requires every raw-airflow consumer,
threshold, axis, diagnostic, and conversion to be classified and migrated as
one cutover. The conditioned `2b30 -> 2b26 -> 6980 -> 6ac0 -> 6ab8 -> 6ab6`
route does not remove that audit or bind `2b26` to physical measured MAF at
runtime. Prefer a characterized sensor/housing that stays truthful within the
stock representation over an unvalidated global rescale.

The alternating accumulators are **32-bit sums of Q6 samples**, not 32-bit
kg/h publications. `00435048` divides by twice the **sample count**, converts
to Q5, clears the consumed bank, and applies the correction gain only when
`1f62 != 0`. The average executes before that availability test and has no
local zero-count guard. Its separate `2b2a` output is
`sat_round(2b26 * 32768 / u16(4 * 30a4))`; denominator narrowing and the
zero-divisor sentinel belong to that derivative, not to shared charge `2ada`.
`ip_pq_sp_puls` and hysteresis update `1e88`, which can inhibit measured-flow
eligibility.

## 2. Measured versus modeled path

`00435180.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) contains the
observable selection:

> an explicit rights decision. The surrounding authored analysis is retained;
> this omission is not replacement instruction-level evidence.
`r13+0x1fea` is an outer raw write gate: when it is nonzero,
`FUN_00435180` leaves `r13+0x2b30` unchanged rather than selecting either
candidate. The structure-730 A2L has `lv_clc_2seg_ena` at this same address,
and its recurrence-switch group projects `c_n_seg_2_ena` into canonical
`FUN_00438d94`; this corroborates a two-segment calculation-enable role without
making the mismatched A2L address map authoritative. Only when that gate permits
an update does `r13+0x1e8c` choose between the two candidates.
This distinction matters in a RAM capture because the retained value may be
stale rather than a newly selected sample.

`r13+0x2b26` participates in the route commonly hypothesized as corrected
measured MAF, while `r13+0x2ac2` is the other selector candidate. The A2L
`SYS_Mass_airflow_variables` calibration group independently projects onto
canonical `FUN_00435048` and `FUN_00435180` and supplies the useful
`maf_kgh_mes`, `maf_kgh_mes_bas`, `maf_mes`, `maf_dif`, `maf_kgh`, and
`maf_mmv` lexicon. It does not bind a specific canonical RAM address. Thus
calling `2b26` measured MAF remains a runtime-contingent interpretation, not an
unconditional physical identity.

Focused `FUN_00435048` adds the previously missed conditioned feedback: when
`r13+0x1f62 == 0`, it copies `2b30` directly into `2b26`. Combined with the
selector, this forms a conditioned cycle: `2b26 -> 2b30` only when
`1fea == 0` and `1e8c != 0`. The other selector edge is
`2ac2 -> 2b30` when `1fea == 0` and `1e8c == 0`.

Whole-program facts still record 35 direct reads of `r13+0x2b30` across 23
functions. The continuation recovers the conditional integrator route into
`r13+0x6b9a` described above. `FUN_00140b88` also writes zero to `2b30` during
initialization; proximity or co-access must not be treated as a dataflow edge.

`00474698.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) shows that
MAF availability/diagnostic state can set `r13+0x1f62`; it is therefore unsafe
to treat the selection as a single user-facing "MAF off" switch.

The source-bound gate producers are in the
[acquisition closure](../analysis/derived/review-2026-09-04-offline-maf-closure/findings.json).
`0046dec4` clears `1e8c` if `1f62==0`, `2266!=0`, `1e88!=0`, or
`2314!=0` together with a status nibble equal to 1. Canonical `c_conf_maf=2`
allows the `00474698` availability latch when `1fe1!=0`; configuration 1
additionally requires the strict ADC-index window `41 < 1f40 < 230` and a
count exceeding 10. Those configuration-1 presence checks are not universal
electrical fault limits. `00438d94` clears `1fea` unless `1fe2!=0` and
`21d8>150`; otherwise it uses `21da & 1`. Physical meaning of the opaque
qualifier/fault records and their live values is not inferred from adjacency.

## 3. Conditioned adjusted-load production

### Raw-state route through `2b26`

`00434c48.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) copies
`r13+0x2b26` into `r13+0x6980`. In
`00442f7c.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition), that value
feeds `r13+0x6ac0`, which is normalized and signed-saturated into
`r13+0x6ab8`. When `CW_EISY` (`cal:xdf:1e000:value`) bit 0 is set, `6ab8`
contributes directly to adjusted load `r13+0x6ab6` with the `6b62` correction.
The complete static route is therefore:

```text
2b30 --when 1f62 == 0--> 2b26 -> 6980 -> 6ac0 -> 6ab8
     --when CW_EISY bit 0 is set--> 6ab6
```

The conditions are part of the claim. Static evidence does not identify the
active runtime producer or prove the physical meaning of `2b26`.

The instructions make the intermediate precision and actual normalization
explicit:

```text
A = u32 state at absolute 0x007fe0f8 (r13+60f8)
Anew = A + (u16(6980) - high16(A)) * u16(K_ZK_MSHFM)
6ac0 = high16(Anew)                  # gain raw64887; Q16 coefficient
N = trunc0(RPM_effective * 31219 / 16384)
6b68 = low16(min(N,65535))
6ab8 = sat_s16(floor(u16(6ac0)*5120/u16(6b68)))
        or 32767 when 6b68==0
```

`00410f20` reads/writes `A` through a pointer. Its integer airflow range
remains 16 bits; the other 16 bits retain fractional precision. With legal
u16 input/gain and u32 accumulator the update stays in the unsigned range;
there is no explicit saturating instruction in the helper.

`004439b4` uses signed `6984` RPM directly when `6eda.bit3` is set, otherwise
`max(RPM,400)`. `6e9b.bit1` gates this normalizer update, and `0016e7a8`
initializes it. The RPM product fits signed 32 bits for the loaded widths;
the normalizer has an upper clamp but no negative-input floor. The acquired
numerator is at most `65535*5120 = 335539200`, so its signed division does
not hide a multiply overflow. Negative RPM is not asserted a valid state.

`00443084..0044308c` subtracts signed `6b62` and performs a plain halfword
store. **The measured branch has neither the modeled branch's 600 floor nor
a post-subtraction upper saturation.** The sole direct `6b62` producer found
in the frozen census is `004439b4`, which writes zero. A hypothetical
sign-crossing correction illustrates a typing hazard, not a demonstrated
running-engine state.

Evidence: normalizer/load instructions (supporting artifact withheld from public edition),
reference producer (supporting artifact withheld from public edition),
and unsigned PT1 (supporting artifact withheld from public edition).

### Complementary modeled-filling branch

`004439b4.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) selects one of
two calculated filling candidates and writes the final state at `r13+0x6b6a`:

> an explicit rights decision. The surrounding authored analysis is retained;

The selector calibration is the exact canonical XDF object `S_EVNN_AUS`
("switch neural network off"). A2L function-group projection supplies a
coherent, revision-aware lexicon: 38 `EISY_EV` calibration anchors map into
canonical `FUN_004439b4`, and that old group exposes `Rfv_ist_nn`,
`Rfv_ist_hub`, and `Rfv_ist`. Therefore `6b9a`, `6b5a`, and `6b6a` are strong
neural-network, lift-based fallback, and selected modeled-unthrottled-relative-

This confirms that the final modeled filling is selected after the upstream
filling and pressure corrections. `KL_RFVIST_MX` is therefore not, by itself,
the final cylinder-filling ceiling.

On the `CW_EISY` bit-0-clear branch,
`00442f7c.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) normalizes
`r13+0x6b6a` into signed-saturated `r13+0x6abe`. The nested choice is now
resolved for the canonical image: `S_RFST_ON` (`0x85e005`, file `0x5e005`)
is zero, disabling both the startup-map alternative `6aba` and its transition
filter. Thus `6abe` is copied directly to `6ade`, then signed correction
`6b62` is subtracted and the `K_RFMIN_EISY` floor applied:

```text
6b6a -> 6abe
6abe -> 6ade           # S_RFST_ON == 0; no startup override or transition PT1
max(s16(6ade) - s16(6b62), 600) -> u16(6ab6)
```

Fresh evidence:
[`load-continuation/findings.json`](../analysis/derived/review-2026-09-04-load-continuation/findings.json),
with canonical-byte hashes, four fresh function exports and full instructions.
The general startup-map predicate is `6eda.bit3 == 0 && S_RFST_ON != 0 &&
(global01803350.bit0 == 0 || S_RFGDST_OFF != 0)`. Its complement uses `6abe`,
with the transition filter additionally requiring `6ae2 < K_TD_RFSTART`
and the same nonzero start-enable/restriction gates. Canonical
`S_RFGDST_OFF=0`, `K_TD_RFSTART=2`, and `S_RFST_ON=0` make both alternatives
inactive for every runtime flag combination. `004430f0` and `00443204`
read the zero enable byte; `00443258` stores the direct result. A retained
source-derived truth table checks all 65,536 combinations of the two flag
bytes; it is not ECU emulation or live data.

The final `6ab6` assignment has a lower bound and a halfword store, not a
second signed upper saturation. Neither that representation nor the recovered
stock selector proves a boosted physical air-mass model.

The canonical `FUN_00442f7c` calibration set projects strongly onto the A2L
`EISY` group (18 matching anchors). Its `Rf` and `Rf_ubeg` outputs make
`r13+0x6ab6` a strong corrected/principal-relative-filling candidate and
`r13+0x6abe` a strong pre-correction relative-filling candidate. Exact
engineering names remain medium confidence; the conditioned raw dataflow and
signed saturation are direct executable evidence.

## 4. The load-to-cylinder-mass conversion

`00434c98.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) reads
`r13+0x6ab6`, the calibration constant `c_maf_ref`, and literal denominator
`10000` (`0x2710`). It calls
`0040b46c.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition), which is a
rounded, saturating multiply/divide helper:

> Firmware implementation excerpt withheld from the public edition pending
> an explicit rights decision. The surrounding authored analysis is retained;
> this omission is not replacement instruction-level evidence.
The cylinder-mass assignment is therefore:

```text
r13+0x2ada raw
    = sat_round((r13+0x6ab6 raw * c_maf_ref raw) / 10000)
```

The stock `c_maf_ref` value is 30503 raw counts. Its XDF conversion is
`0.021195 * X`, giving **646.511085 mg/stk**. If `r13+0x6ab6` uses the
observed 0.01-percent load convention, the physical relationship is:

```text
cylinder mass mg/stk ~= load_percent * 646.511085 / 100
```

Illustrative values from the stock reference constant are:

| Adjusted load | Derived cylinder mass |
|---:|---:|
| 100.00% | 646.5 mg/stk |
| 108.36% | 700.6 mg/stk |
| 115.00% | 743.5 mg/stk |
| 135.00% | 872.8 mg/stk |
| 154.00% | 995.8 mg/stk |

The last row is only a scale illustration; it is not an 8-psi calibration
target. Volumetric efficiency, temperature, exhaust backpressure, valve events,
pressure corrections, and the active load representation determine the real
value.

The `r13+0x2ada` output remains unsigned 16-bit at the firmware/XDF convention
of `0.021195 mg/stk/count`. **65535 is the saturating helper's sentinel, not
a validated usable charge.** Its numeric equivalent is 1389.014325 mg/stk;
the largest non-sentinel code is 65534, or 1388.99313 mg/stk. With stock
`c_maf_ref`, load 21484 produces charge 65533 and load **21485 produces
65535**. Thus this load-to-charge conversion clips before the upstream
327.67% normalization ceiling, even though it has more range than the stock
fuel/spark/torque map axes.

The load read at `00434d50` is `LHZ`, not `LHA`. The same stored bits are
signed in the other 36 direct `6ab6` reads. Any future change allowing
negative/wrapped final load must therefore address consumer interpretation;
calling the shared variable simply `int16` or simply `uint16` hides a real
boundary.

### Explicit conversions, saturation and hold boundaries

The transition inventory (supporting artifact withheld from public edition)
retains exact instruction ranges. The main distinctions are:

| Boundary | Behavior and consequence |
|---|---|
| ADC/table | Byte truncation and direct u16 table read, not extrapolation. Stock indices 233–255 all hold 65535. |
| Accumulation/averaging | u16 → u32 addition; count and sum stores wrap, not saturate. Valid counts up to 65535 keep `sum <= 65535²`; a count rollover or empty bank is a separate scheduler risk. Division floors; average narrows to u16 and changes Q6 to Q5. |
| Correction | u16 × u8 fits u32; `>>7` then u16 saturation. `0040831c` holds the airflow axis above 800 kg/h and temperature outside −20.25…99.75°C. A gain cannot recover clipped sensor information. |
| PT1 | u32 fractional accumulator → high u16. More precision, not more integer airflow range. |
| Normalization | u16 airflow → signed 32-bit numerator/division → signed saturation ±32768/32767. Zero denominator returns +32767. |
| Final load | Subtract signed correction in 32 bits; plain halfword narrowing. Only modeled branch has a lower floor; neither assignment adds an upper clamp. |
| Charge | Zero-extend load; rounded u16 multiply/divide; saturate to `ffff`. Multiplication plus half-divisor fits u32 for these halfword arguments. No recovered uniform consumer-side sentinel rejection. |
| Charge derivatives | Signed saturated difference, optionally divided by two; previous-charge global updated. `2b32` copies rapidly rising charge or applies rounded Q16 filtering with a minimum one-count step. The outer selector gate holds these states too. |
| Consumer lookups | Endpoint hold preserves the final row/column, not the slope. It is a calibration-domain limit, not a shared-variable clamp. |

### Optional integrator: a separate architectural ~1024 kg/h limit

`00447004` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition)
contains a second, independent airflow range boundary:

```text

6c72 = sat_s16(6c6e + floor(u16(6e52)/80)
                       + floor(u16(global01802dd8)*32/10))

6c76 = sat_u16(6c78 + s16(6c74))
6c7a = sat_u16(u16(63c6) - K_DPS_INTMN)
6c7c = min(6c76,6c7a)
6c6a = max(6c7c,K_PSMIN)
```

The complete gain-side states `6c82/6c8a/6c86/6c88/6c84`, copies
`6c7e/6c80`, and pressure-difference output `6c6c` are in the inventory.
The gain path includes halfword narrowing and zero-denominator sentinel
branches; its full valid runtime domain is not proved here.

On the acquired Q5 convention, signed `6c72` clips **total inflow** at
`32767/32 = 1023.96875 kg/h`; positive additive flows consume part of that
range. Pressure subsequently remains upper-bounded by the ambient-derived
`63c6` chain. With bit 2 clear, pressure reaches `6ac2`, the NN
pressure/temperature product `6b9a`, and selected modeled `6b6a`. Thus a local
MAF-table redesign alone does **not** extend this optional modeled-pressure
route. Do not confuse its signed **airflow** clamp with `6ab8`'s signed
**relative-load** clamp.

## 6. Interpolation does not extrapolate

The table helpers are normal piecewise-linear interpolators *inside* their
axes. Their out-of-range branches explicitly return an endpoint cell:

- `00414978.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) handles
  signed 16-bit table values used by MDIOP and other maps;
- `00415070.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) handles
  signed 8-bit values used by the spark maps;
- `00408ab4.c` (supporting artifact withheld from public edition) handles the
  base-lambda family layout.

For an input above the last breakpoint, these functions choose the last row or
column. They do not continue the slope beyond the table. Therefore the stock
firmware can calculate a value above a map's final axis while the map quietly
holds its last naturally aspirated cell.

This behavior proves that raising only a load limiter is insufficient. The
conditioned raw-state path shows how `2b26` can reach these tables through
`6ab6`, but it does not establish that a higher-range physical MAF supplies
`2b26` or that the relevant `1f62` and `CW_EISY` conditions are active.

## What the table code permits—and does not prove

If the active selectors and internal states accept the required boosted range,
the existing fixed-size maps can be extended by changing their upper axis
breakpoints and supplying corresponding data. This trades some high-load axis
resolution for additional range but does not require changing the interpolation
function or table dimensions.

A future high-load implementation must evaluate at least:

1. runtime binding of physical measured MAF to `2b26`, plus observation of the
   active `1f62`, `1fea`, `1e8c`, and `CW_EISY` branches;
2. the canonical modeled producer and its pressure authority; the recovered
   bit-1 airflow/integrator route is a separate configuration, not a prerequisite
   for validating the stock load topology;
3. the truthful MAF transfer and its correction/plausibility domain;
4. pressure/filling corrections and limits;
5. every active normal, safe, throttled, and full-load spark path;
6. all active base-lambda families and full-load enrichment logic;
7. MDIOP, torque ceilings, driver demand, and torque monitors;
8. airflow-indexed knock thresholds; and
9. component protection and relevant diagnostics.

The new high-load cells should not all be generated with one mathematical
rule:

- Lambda values are deliberate boosted targets, not an extrapolation of the
  NA slope.
- Spark should start conservative and be validated from measured torque,
  temperature, and knock behavior.
- Torque-model values can be seeded from air mass and measured torque, then
  reconciled with monitors and intervention behavior.
- Pressure corrections require the actual pressure and filling relationship.
- Knock thresholds must retain detection authority and be validated with the
  new engine acoustics.

## Remaining blockers

The decompilation proves a conditioned raw-state route to adjusted load, but it
does not prove that physical measured MAF owns `2b26` at runtime or that
calibration-only changes are sufficient. Remaining gates are:

- runtime binding of physical MAF to the active `1f62` acquisition producer;
- validation of the selected load architecture: canonical modeled load or a
  separately reviewed, source-bound direct-MAF configuration;
- truthful pressure/temperature inputs and validated actual-charge behavior on
  the modeled route. The conditional airflow/integrator bridge is recovered,
  but the stock pressure ceiling and sensor interface remain the relevant gates;
- the `id_maf_tab` 1023.984375 kg/h representation limit;
- the exact physical MAF and housing transfer curve;
- pressure-correction axes that currently stop near atmospheric pressure;
- active selector identification for each spark/lambda/torque family;
- upstream saturation and signed-state behavior at the intended load;
- pressure, airflow, torque, and diagnostic plausibility;
- checksum/signature-safe write and recovery testing.

The original 1024 kg/h ceiling is local to the Q6 table. A redesign preserving
downstream Q5 units is distinct from a global airflow-unit rescale; neither is
authorized here. The optional signed-Q5 integrator and shared charge sentinel
are additional architectural limits. The static route and integer experiment
do not validate a sensor/housing, correction curve, installed branch, or
physical high-load behavior.
